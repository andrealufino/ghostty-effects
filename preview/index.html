Use it

      

      

        
gfx off and gfx on switch everything while keeping the selection; gfx auto on pauses the effects on battery.

        
Settings are the constants at the top of each shader. Edit, then run gfx on to reload Ghostty.

        
While an animated shader is active, Ghostty redraws the focused window continuously. That is the only real cost.
